How it works

1

AI watches your screen

The ScentBar's AI vision model analyzes what's happening on your monitor in real time.

2

Labels match scents

Each preset slot defines a few keywords. When the AI sees one with high confidence, the matching scent fires.

3

You smell the game

Real natural-oil scents — gunpowder, forest, rain, leather — released at the perfect moment.
